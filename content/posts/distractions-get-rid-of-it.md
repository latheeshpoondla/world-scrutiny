We already know about distraction and its causes. So now we are going to know the solution for the distraction.

### *Phases to get rid of Distraction*s:

Self-Control is the only solution for this problem. There is only a small difference between a great person and a normal person, and that is self-control.

*We are all distracted from our goals,* not because of lack of experience, we are distracted because of the *inability to act on the experience due to uncontrolled mind and senses.*

Technology really increases our ability to control external affairs. Everything is in our hands. We can control TV with a remote, we can control the temperature of the room with AC(Air-Conditioner), we can control a vehicle with steering and go in light speed, and we program, organize, manage anything with software & Hardware technology we have now. We can control any external things but not our minds. Most of the time when we are using this technology, we are getting distracted. If we don’t have self-control, technology will change our entire character. Now, we can think of *the fact that “we are not controlling the technology, technology is controlling us”.*

### Action-Reaction:

A Train Driver asks the passengers, “*Dear passengers I have two news, one is good & other is bad, which one should I say first?”.* Everyone replied, for a good one. Then he said “*We are going at very high speed that anyone has never gone. This will be an all-time record”* Everyone cheered up.

Then he said with a sad voice “*And the bad news is, we are out of control about the train. We may crash any time.”*

We don’t know which option to deny. Everything seems to be exciting, demanding our attention. This puts our minds under stress. Our Attachment to the Physical Body (External Self) also increases. But when we misidentify with the external self it causes neglect of Values.

1.  So, coming to the point as our post title, the first step to get rid of distraction is to fix our mind with a strong will as ” *I will do anything to be distraction-free*“. You fill your mind like that. Think about why I am saying this much strongly (with experience). *It is the most difficult to stay focused.* At first, we will think we are focused on our goals. But, just after a small interval of time, we get distracted easily at the beginning stages of focusing on our goals.
2.  And the second step is to decide which response is right and which is wrong for an action. We are called by someone, or a WhatsApp ping, or a Facebook post, …etc. are called stimulus. *An event that attracts our attention is called stimulus.* And we choose a response for the stimulus. And the decision is made in ways one is by our external senses and the other is by our soul.

![](/WS_Media/Post%20Media/body.jpeg)

*Our Body contains five sense organs they are eyes, ears, tongue, nose, skin. Mind choices a decision for the stimulus to get instant pleasures. This way of response is the cause of distractions, and these responses make us far from our goals.*

To not be like that we have to make a response by the decision of our soul.

*Our Soul has certain values. For example, animals don’t wear dresses, but we do, animals eat food wherever it is from when they are hungry, but we don’t, they kill another living being for food, but we should not – “I will talk about this point later”.*

All the above comparison is between animals and us. That’s the point, we human beings have some values for our soul but they don’t. That’s why we are different from other animals. By following the decisions made by the external senses we totally lose our control on the response to a stimulus. That’s why it is difficult to stay focused on our goals.
