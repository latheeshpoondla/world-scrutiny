### ***1\. Distraction is a choice:***

Distraction is a common enemy for everyone and for any generation. The object, person, or thought – any work which diverts our attention is called a distraction. The Secret of success is “Focus & Commitment”.

We should always focus on the goal or work that we are doing and commit not to get distracted from the work. Some may think “I haven’t been distracted until now”. But they were wrong, everyone was distracted in their life and also will distract again and again.

Examples:

1.  A boy was studying for his exams. Suddenly a sound came from T.V that his mother was watching his favorite program. Then his thoughts were all diverted to T.V. He couldn’t study well for his exams. He was doing so many mistakes while reading and writing. He was distracted.
2.  A boy was watching his favorite T.V program on the day before his exams. His parents forced him to study for exams. He opened his books, but he couldn’t read letters all he could read was the T.V program. He was distracted.

*Technology is not bad. It depends upon the mindset of the user who uses it.*

Technology affects our human value. It gives us instant pleasure. And this desire for immediate pleasure declines our human values.

***Our Existence:***

There are three levels of existence – External Self, Intermediate Self, Inner Self. The external self is the physical body that helps us to interact with our surroundings. It has five senses namely eyes, ears, skin, tongue, nose. Through which we perceive the stimulus.

We perceive the stimulus through the external self, and we process the information and give response through the decision-making mechanism called Intermediate Self or Conscience.

The Conscience formulates a response based on our values. These values come from our inner self. The inner self is the core level of existence. This is also called the real self.

By experiencing the instant pleasure of the techno-gadgets. Our conscience neglects the values of our inner self. To the degree, conscience is governed by the external self. This makes the inner self unhealthy.

*In a healthy state of the inner self, our conscience makes conscious choices – those choices are based on the values of our inner self. In the unhealthy state of our inner self, our conscience makes unconscious choices. This leads to distraction. Such unconscious choices are influenced by our external self-i.e. by our instincts.*

***The painkiller effect:***

Technology functions the same as a painkiller. A painkiller will not cure a disease, it will just reduce the pain. For this reason, painkillers are cheap, and medicine is costly.

Technology only gives us instant pleasure. It makes us feel we are fine. But actually, we are not fine. The inner self-containing the values like honesty, rightfulness…. etc. will be lost.

For example, our body gives pain when we are attacked by a disease or in time of injury. Then our doctors give us painkillers(anesthesia), then we may think the injury is cured because of no pain. But you are still cured. If you won’t recognize the effect of painkillers, then you will die after some days with that injury or disease.

In the same way, we have to obey real human values even though it is tough, and we should always be with our healthy state of the inner self.
